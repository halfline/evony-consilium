Privacy policy · Terms of use

Select a player in Players or a keep on the map to edit its name, account ID, level, castle tier, or position. Castle footprints follow their configured size. Drag a keep to a free grid location. Locked keeps are excluded from automatic placement.

The highest keep starts as rally setter. Hover or hold another keep to reveal its star, then use Arrange formation above the map. Keeps are ranked by level, with higher levels placed nearer the setter. Alliance buildings and blocked tiles are avoided.

Changes save automatically every 30 seconds while this team is open and the page is active. Save team immediately shares maps, roster membership, and team settings. Save player shares that player's profile, including appearance and statistics. Player drafts are kept on this device when you navigate away. Undo applies to team edits. Keep level defaults to 42 and culture to Japan.

Appearance: bundled images are extracted original Evony sprites. The default grid uses a 2:1 diamond and is adjustable. Battlefield geography, coordinate extents, footprints, and premium skin names are not fully verified. Imported map screenshots and custom sprites can supply missing current content.

Asset source: the official browser client references this public Unity bundle index. Extraction tooling and provenance are included in the repository. Bundles may predate the live game; public availability does not grant redistribution rights.

Evony graphics © Top Games Inc. This is an unofficial planning tool. Account IDs are stored locally with your plan.

Keyboard: Ctrl/Cmd Z undo, Esc cancel, +/− zoom, arrow keys move the selected unlocked keep one tile. Shift-drag selects a group; Ctrl/Command-click toggles individual objects. Delete removes selected objects from the map.
